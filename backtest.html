How to read this
R = the amount risked on one trade. Avg R above 0 means the idea made money on average. Profit factor (PF) above 1 means wins were bigger than losses.

The luck check
Under each idea, "Luck range" shows what 40 random-entry tries earned, using the same stops, targets and costs. An idea only counts as better than luck if its all-years Avg R beats the top 5% of those random tries. This also cancels out any bias in how stops are filled, because random entries get the same treatment.

An idea PASSES only if all of these are true
Avg R above 0.05 and PF above 1.1 in both the early and later periods, at least 40 trades in each, and it beats the luck range.

Limits
Daily reference rates have one price per day, so stops and targets are checked only at each day's close and fill at their exact level. Overnight swap costs are not included and could matter for USD/ZAR. Spreads were measured at one moment and move during the day. With six ideas tested, about one could look good by pure luck, so a pass is a reason to test further, never a promise or a 70% win rate.
