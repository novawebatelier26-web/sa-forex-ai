How to read this
R = the amount risked on one trade. A loss is about -1R, a win is +2R (take-profit is twice the stop-loss). With 2:1 reward-to-risk you only need about 33% wins to break even, so the win rate alone does not decide whether a strategy is good. Average R and the worst drop matter more.

Limits of this test
Daily reference rates give one price per day, with no highs or lows, so stop-loss and take-profit are only checked at each day's closing price. Spreads are assumed (EUR/USD 1.2 pips, GBP/USD 1.5 pips, USD/ZAR 50 pips) and overnight swap costs are not included. Trades open for at most 20 days. The rules were not tuned to this data. Past results do not predict the future, and a 70% win rate is a target, not a promise.
