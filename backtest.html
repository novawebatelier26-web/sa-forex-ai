How to read this
R = the amount risked on one trade. Avg R above 0 means the idea made money on average. Profit factor (PF) above 1 means total wins were bigger than total losses. An idea only passes if both periods show Avg R above 0.05 and PF above 1.1 with at least 40 trades each.

Limits of this test
Daily reference rates have one price per day, so stops and targets are only checked at each day's close. Spreads are assumed (EUR/USD 1.2 pips, GBP/USD 1.5, USD/ZAR 50) and overnight swap costs are not included. Entries happen at the day's closing price. With five ideas tested, one can pass by luck, so a pass is a reason to test further, never a promise. The "Random entries" row is a sanity check: it shows what luck plus costs produces.
